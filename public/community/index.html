Community guidelines

Keep conversations practical, respectful, and safe.

Protect your tenant

Never publish passwords, PFX files, private keys, app secrets, access tokens, real tenant IDs, internal URLs, personal data, or unredacted logs and screenshots.

Make questions reproducible

Include the relevant product, expected result, actual error, and steps already tried. Replace names and identifiers with fictional examples.

Be constructive

Explain what worked, credit sources, keep feedback respectful, and mark the useful answer or follow-up where possible.

Share safe code

Remove credentials and environment-specific details from scripts. Review commands for destructive actions before sharing or running them.
